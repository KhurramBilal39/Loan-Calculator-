Loan EMI Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

What we collect

Loan EMI Calculator does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.

What stays on your device

The numbers you type into the calculator are used only on your device to work out the result. They are not sent anywhere and are not saved. Nothing is stored on a server.

Third parties

The app does not send your data to any third party.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
